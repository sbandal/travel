# Oʻahu Thanksgiving Week Trip Planner (Nov 24–30, 2026)

Interactive 7-day Oʻahu vacation planner built for a Thanksgiving week stay at **Hotel Renew (Waikiki)** with flights **Southwest WN3882** (Tue 11/24) and **WN2026** (Mon 11/30).

## Features
- **Interactive Oʻahu Map & Day-by-Day Itinerary**: Color-coded pins for all 7 days, driving/walking route lines, day & category filters, and click-to-fly synchronization between map and itinerary cards.
- **Oʻahu Local Food Guide**: Curated poke, malasadas, garlic shrimp, shave ice, plate lunch, and dinner spots with one-click **Show on map**.
- **Thanksgiving Week Bookings & Checklist**: Persistent `localStorage` checklist highlighting holiday closures (Polynesian Cultural Center & Pearl Harbor) and time-sensitive booking windows (Hanauma Bay 2-day 7:00 AM HST window, Diamond Head).
- **Car Rental & Waikiki Parking Comparator**: Interactive calculator comparing the **3-Day Mid-Trip Car (Thu–Sat)** vs. **6-Day Full-Trip Car** at the **$31.50/day** employer rate, paired with **Waikiki Banyan (~$30/night)** vs. **Hotel Renew Valet ($60 + tax/night)**.
